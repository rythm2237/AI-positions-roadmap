# Direct Company Source Expansion

The Job Agent prioritizes direct, public company ATS sources for technology-oriented employers before generic aggregators.

## Phase 1 priorities

1. Software / SaaS / AI / cloud / developer platforms
2. Fintech with substantial engineering and automation teams
3. Travel-tech / marketplace technology companies
4. Other digital-first employers with relevant AI, automation, data, cloud and business-systems roles

## Source admission rule

A company may be added to the direct registry only after its public Greenhouse or Lever board is verified to expose current vacancies. Direct-source results are marked `sourceConfidence: high` and must still pass the normal country, eligibility and verification pipeline.

## Current expansion batch

The initial four-company seed is expanded with Dataiku, Raisin, GetYourGuide, Contentful, Contentsquare, BlaBlaCar and Back Market. All are technology/digital-first employers and have public ATS boards verified during the expansion work.

The registry remains intentionally curated. Automated company discovery will be introduced separately so newly discovered employers can be validated before being promoted to a direct source.
